# HouseCat — cat virtual pet for iPhone (black-cat mascot)

Spec: @docs/SPEC.md (every number is a locked decision). Art inventory: @docs/ASSETS.md (assets are finished and confirmed before the code build; build against the real files). Content brief: @docs/CONTENT.md (lines, names, objectives, mini-game rules are generated from it as data). Art rules: @docs/STYLE.md. Keep @docs/ARCHITECTURE.md current. The name is HouseCat (chosen 17 Oct 2026). Product IDs use the `housecat.` prefix and are created once, correctly: App Store Connect never lets them be renamed.

## What this is
A painterly 2D pet game. Every visit starts outside the player's home in real weather; they tap the door, the cat (with a chosen personality) greets them; they feed, pet, play, collect gifts and furnish rooms the cat uses. Adults 25–40 first; rated 4+, not Kids Category. No ads. Full scope in v1: cat builder, world map with search and seasons, 8 houses, 6 rooms, 80 furniture, widgets with the visit streak (including the home-model widget), 3 mini-games, season pass (free + paid tracks), groups, co-owning and cat-sitting via CloudKit sharing, gifting, the stray, HealthKit layer, Siri/Shortcuts, Watch complication, iPad dollhouse view, Mac menu-bar cat, wallpaper and share-card exports. There is no timeline: work runs as ordered steps, each with an exit test (SPEC.md "Build phases"); nothing is staged out.

## Targets and layout

Minimum OS: iOS/iPadOS 26, watchOS 26, macOS 26 (decided 17 Oct 2026; build with Xcode 26). Bundle id `com.housecatgame.housecat`.
- `Widgets/` WidgetKit extension: small/medium/large (home model)/Lock Screen/StandBy widgets, App Intents (Feed, Pet, Visit), Live Activity (Nap timer). App Group `group.com.housecatgame.housecat`.
- `Watch/`, `Mac/` (menu-bar app), iPad layouts inside `App/`: added as their own steps; all read HouseCatCore through the shared CloudKit save.
- `Packages/HouseCatCore/` pure Swift package: models, `Tuning.swift`, cat brain, economy, levels, `DaySeed`, widget state. Owns every rule. Never imports UIKit or SpriteKit. 100% of rules have unit tests.
- `Content/` `lines.json`, `catalogue.json`, `gifts.json`, `outfits.json`, `objectives.json`, `seasons.json`, `minigames.json` (data, not code; generated from docs/CONTENT.md, decoded by `ContentBundle` in HouseCatCore and validated by `ContentValidationTests`). `events.json` comes with the event system.
- `assets/` the finished art masters (delivered 17 Oct 2026): furniture, room tiles and light maps, cat puppet parts, the cat-builder art (`assets/cat/custom`: white bases, masks, `catbuilder.json`), outfits, world, gifts, UI, widget frames, audio. `assets/manifest.json` lists every file with its class and conventions; docs/ASSETS.md explains how each class is used. `tools/app-assets/build_app_art.py` copies or downsizes what the app draws into `App/Art` (with `art.json`: each file's painted size, its downscale and the reference room furniture was painted in); never edit the masters. `tools/asset-pipeline/` holds the Python that made them (re-cut, re-key, re-tile).
- `Packages/HouseCatModels/` SwiftData persistence of the event log (`EventStore`); `Packages/HouseCatUI/` shared SwiftUI views; `Packages/HouseCatGame/` SpriteKit scenes; `Packages/HouseCatStore/` the `EntitlementService` protocol with its StoreKit 2 and RevenueCat implementations, the product table (`StoreCatalog`) and the Keychain mirror.
- `Store/HouseCat.storekit` the local StoreKit configuration (every launch product), generated by `tools/store/build_storekit.py` from `Content/`; the HouseCat scheme runs with it. `AppTests/` StoreKit tests with `SKTestSession`, hosted in the app.
- `project.yml` (xcodegen) is the source of truth for the Xcode project; never hand-edit the pbxproj.

## Commands
- Generate project: `xcodegen generate` (the .xcodeproj is generated and git-ignored)
- App sounds from `assets/audio`: `tools/app-assets/build_app_audio.sh` (afconvert to AAC into `App/Audio`)
- App icon and launch logo from `assets/brand`: `tools/app-assets/build_app_assets.sh` (writes `Shared/AppIcon.icon` and the LaunchLogo image set; never edit the masters)
- Build without XcodeBuildMCP: `xcodebuild -project HouseCat.xcodeproj -scheme HouseCat -destination 'platform=iOS Simulator,name=iPhone 18 Pro' -derivedDataPath ~/Library/Developer/Xcode/DerivedData/HouseCat-CLI build` (schemes: HouseCat for iPhone, iPad, widgets and Watch; HouseCatMac). Never build into the repo: it sits in ~/Desktop, and simulator apps then hang on macOS's Desktop-folder privacy check when loading frameworks
- Build/run/screenshot: XcodeBuildMCP tools in the iPhone and iPad simulators (Josh tests in the simulators, no physical device yet; widgets, Live Activities and the Watch app all run in simulators too)
- Core tests: `cd Packages/HouseCatCore && swift test` (every package under `Packages/` has its own `swift test`)
- StoreKit tests: `xcodebuild test -project HouseCat.xcodeproj -scheme HouseCat -destination 'platform=iOS Simulator,name=iPhone 18 Pro' -derivedDataPath ~/Library/Developer/Xcode/DerivedData/HouseCat-CLI -collect-test-diagnostics never` (the app skips StoreKit at launch when it hosts tests, so `SKTestSession` can load the configuration first)
- If a StoreKit test hangs (usually the outfit purchase) the simulator is holding a stale sandbox sign-in prompt from a command-line launch: `xcrun simctl shutdown <udid> && xcrun simctl boot <udid>` and run again.
- Regenerate the StoreKit configuration after changing outfits, sets or seasons: `python3 -I tools/store/build_storekit.py`
- Format and lint: `swiftformat .` then `swiftlint`
- App art after changing what the game draws (SETS in the script) or any master it uses: `python3 -I tools/app-assets/build_app_art.py` (standard library and `sips`)
- Art tools in `tools/asset-pipeline/` need Python with Pillow, numpy and scipy (rembg for furniture cut-outs): `puppet.py` (rig by eye), `living_room.py` (room layout and scale by eye), `scenes.py` (exterior scenes: Higgsfield requests, keying, door points), `extract.py` (grey-room furniture cut-outs)

## Architecture rules (always)
- The save is an append-only EVENT LOG (fed at t, petted, placed item x, bought y, claimed tier n …). Every piece of state (trust, XP, Beans, streak, room contents, pass progress) is derived from the events + `DaySeed`; nothing is stored as a mutable counter. iPhone, widget, Watch, iPad and Mac all append events to the same iCloud save and never conflict. Compaction keeps a snapshot + the events since it.
- All state derives from timestamps + `DaySeed`, never from running timers; the app and the widget must compute the same state independently.
- Clock rules: a streak day is the local calendar day at the player's wake time; the sky uses the home's time zone; a backwards clock jump is ignored, a forward jump is capped at one day per real day.
- Scenes and widgets read state; they never compute rules. Rules live in HouseCatCore with a test before a screen.
- Every tunable constant lives in `Tuning.swift`. Never inline a magic number.
- Local-first: SwiftData in the App Group, CloudKit private-database mirroring, for the player's own save only. No accounts, no login, no backend.
- Sharing between people (co-owning, groups, cat-sitting, gift links) does NOT go through SwiftData: it uses raw CloudKit records in a shared zone (`CKShare`), small JSON documents (profile card, house layout, cat parameters, event deltas). Design in docs/ARCHITECTURE.md before the first line.
- Weather: fetch WeatherKit on arrival at most once an hour per home, cache three hours, show the Apple Weather attribution and link where weather is displayed. Widgets never fetch; they read the cached forecast.
- Places: the world map is a stylised painted map (blue sea, green land, no real terrain). Place names and search come from a bundled open place list (GeoNames) with time zones; no Apple Maps data is drawn on it. The pin's coordinate generalises to one of the 8 biomes (city if near a listed city, else coast / countryside / mountain / forest / desert / tropical / snow from a coarse mask).
- Network only for RevenueCat, CloudKit, WeatherKit, PostHog, Sentry. Widgets never touch the network.
- Placeholder art (coloured shapes with correct footprints) is always acceptable; never block on a painting.
- No third-party dependency without asking. Allowed: RevenueCat, PostHog, Sentry.
- Commit after every working step with a one-line message; keep the build green.

## Product rules (never break)
- The cat never dies, never runs away, never loses trust, XP, Beans or items. A missed day = sulk animation + lines + no gift. Nothing else.
- Trust = count of fed days per cat (tiers 3/7/14/30/60/100). Level = player XP (thresholds in Tuning). Both only rise.
- Streak = consecutive days with a VISIT (an arrival in the app, or Feed/Pet from a widget). One free miss per rolling week; pause mode up to 14 days a quarter; a lost streak restarts at 1 and the longest streak is kept. Milestones 7/30/100/365. The streak number and the "visited today?" cat pose are on every widget (SPEC.md "Widgets, the Duolingo layer").
- Cats are built in the cat builder: body (shorthair or longhair with the weight slider, Persian, Siamese, Maine Coon, Sphynx), ears, tail, any coat colour, pattern, white markings, eye colours (odd eyes allowed), eye size, nose, whiskers, age, or a one-tap preset. Midnight, the black cat, is the mascot, the default and the first preset (decided 17 Oct 2026: full customisation replaces "all cats are black cats"). Every field is re-editable later for free. Every builder option is free at launch; extra cats are the paid item (`housecat.cat.slot<n>`). The player always names their own cat; preset names name the look. Looks are composed from the WHITE part sets in `assets/cat/custom` + pattern / white / feature masks + the coat-recipe shader + scales, never separate paintings per look. `catbuilder.json` is the data; `tools/asset-pipeline/tint.py` is the reference the shader must match (SPEC.md "Coat recipe").
- Time together: the cat's profile shows days together since adoption and the cat's age, derived from the adoption event and `DaySeed` (no stored counter). It only ever rises: misses, lost streaks and pause never touch it. Gotcha day on each anniversary (SPEC.md "Age and time together").
- Onboarding order is fixed: landing page → cat creation → geography (painted world map with MapKit place search, then season) → house selection and design → first arrival.
- The home's pin decides terrain (biome mask), local time (time zone), weather and hemisphere season. Season = Auto or a fixed season; a fixed season simulates weather from the biome's seasonal table instead of WeatherKit. No CoreLocation anywhere.
- The exterior is ONE layered scene (sky in code, terrain painted per biome per season at noon, house painted once at noon, overlays, weather and season particles), colour-graded in code for time of day, and every room's window renders that same scene through a mask. Never paint per time of day.
- The arrival in the room view is live: the cat notices you, runs towards the camera (front view, scaled up along the run), rubs along the bottom edge (side view crossing, purr, haptic), then picks its next activity from the behaviour table (mood × weather × personality → an affordance + a pose) and walks to the furniture that has it. Rooms are alive: items carry a `motion` entry (sway, bob, glow, spin, frames) and the room runs idle life (dust in the sunbeam, rain on the glass, steam, the lamp at dusk).
- First arrival: the house is EMPTY apart from the cat. One room open; a housewarming allowance (Tuning: 150 Beans) furnishes it. Every further room needs a level AND Beans above the allowance (Tuning: bedroom L2+200, kitchen L3+300, garden/balcony L6+800, study L9+1,500, attic L12+2,500); money never skips a level (Cat Club halves the Bean cost only). Locked rooms show as lit silhouettes with level and price.
- Beans are earned only, never sold, never expire. The daily gift is earned only and never purchasable (no loot-box rules apply). Nothing random is ever sold. No player-to-player marketplace; duplicate earned gifts can be swapped inside a group or sold back for Beans.
- Paid items are deterministic, priced in money, shown with their real price in the catalogue. No weekly plans. No countdown timers except an event's or season's real end date (`events.json`).
- Season pass: four-week seasons, 20 tiers, free track and paid track; pass points from daily actions (Tuning); buying mid-season unlocks tiers already earned; Cat Club includes the paid track; every reward fixed and visible from day one. The paid track is sold as a NON-RENEWING SUBSCRIPTION (`housecat.pass.<season>`), the product type Apple names for a battle pass, with the end date stated before purchase; items earned on it are kept forever. Non-renewing subscriptions are not restored by StoreKit, so the entitlement is written to the iCloud save (event log) and restored from there.
- The stray: a second cat appears at the window on a monthly cadence from `DaySeed` (weekly with 3+ cats), only after a second room is open and furnished; adopting needs a spare bed in a room beyond the first; look is deterministic from the seed; never adopted = comes back another month. Adoption is `housecat.cat.slot<n>`.
- Groups: up to 12 members via a CloudKit share; profile cards (cat params, house layout, level, streak, personality) as small JSON rendered locally; read-only visits, one paw print a day; cat-sitting (feed a member's cat up to 14 days) and gift links live inside groups. No public map, no free-text chat, no backend. No leaderboards, rankings or competitions anywhere (that alone would move the age rating from 4+ to 13+); the only user-authored text visible to others is the cat's name, passed through a profanity filter. Every group has Leave, Remove member (owner) and Report (emails safety@) — the Online Safety Act treats a group as a small user-to-user service.
- Sharing mechanics: invite, gift and cat-sitting links are one-time CloudKit share URLs (`CKShare.Participant.oneTimeURLParticipant`, never `publicPermission`), one slot per invitee, unaccepted slots expire after 48 h; the share owner is the only writer of anything valuable; each member's authoritative state stays in their own private zone and the group zone holds only small presence cards that can be rebuilt. Player-chosen display names, not iCloud identities (iOS 26 hides participant names without a special entitlement).
- Gifting: gift SKUs are CONSUMABLES (`housecat.gift.*`; a non-consumable can be bought once per Apple Account, a gift can be bought many times); the purchase creates a Gift record shared read-only to one one-time participant; it becomes `redeemed` only on the recipient's acceptance; never re-giftable or exchangeable (Apple 3.1.1); the recipient's entitlement is keyed to their iCloud save so it restores on their other devices. The recipient's cat delivers the wrapped item at the next arrival. Gift tab in the shop, never a prompt.
- Founders' pack (`housecat.founders`, non-consumable): on sale from LAUNCH DAY for 30 days, then gone for good with the end date shown. It cannot be sold in TestFlight (TestFlight purchases are free and do not carry over), so TestFlight testers receive a free offer code for it at launch.
- Entitlements are derived on launch from verified StoreKit transactions (`.verified` only, `Transaction.currentEntitlements`, RevenueCat `verificationResult == .failed` ⇒ no entitlement), held in memory, mirrored to the Keychain for offline use, and NEVER written to the synced store (a share participant could otherwise mint items). Revoked or refunded transactions strip the item on the next launch.
- Trusted time: never trust `Date()` alone for streaks, gifts or cooldowns; anchor on CloudKit `modificationDate`, StoreKit `signedDate` and system uptime; a wall clock that jumps backwards or more than a day ahead of the anchor pauses game time instead of punishing.
- Exports (wallpaper, streak cards, Sunday card) render on device from the current state. Wallpaper options are the player's choice; the app mark is on by default and can be turned off. No time-lapse.
- The widget scene is the cat sitting in the window: the player's cat (its look, back or side view) in their house's window frame, with the real weather and time of day behind the glass, streak and "visited today?" pose on top. No room rendering in the widget; the window frame per house and the cat poses are pre-rendered PNGs in the App Group.
- Other surfaces share HouseCatCore and the iCloud save: Watch complication, iPad dollhouse view, Mac menu-bar cat (Feed/Pet there count as the visit), Siri/Shortcuts via App Intents. Every surface reads state; none computes rules.
- Personality (Aloof, Velcro, Gremlin, Loaf) changes lines and behaviour weights only; it never changes economy numbers.
- Health data (steps, sleep, workouts) is opt-in on day 3 and only ever rewards. It is presented as passive rewards ("your cat walks with you"), never as exercise advice, goals or recommendations (that answer moves the age rating to 9+). Raw samples never leave the device: only derived flags ("walk goal met") sync, in `allowsCloudEncryption` fields; no health value ever reaches analytics (Apple 5.1.3). Request each HealthKit type only when its feature is first switched on.
- Notifications are local, user-scheduled, opt-in via a pre-permission screen after the first feed. Season and event announcements are marketing and have their own separate opt-in toggle (Apple 4.5.4).
- Analytics (PostHog, EU-hosted) is on by default with a one-tap opt-out in onboarding and Settings; anonymous IDs only, no session replay, Sentry PII scrubbing on. A "Delete all my data" action wipes the local store, the CloudKit zones and the vendor records by the anonymous ID shown in Settings; "Export my data" shares a JSON of the save.
- Text boxes and meows only; no voice. Lines come from `lines.json`, never hard-coded.

- iPad: launch screen required, all orientations and resizable scenes (no `UIRequiresFullScreen`). Mac: App Sandbox on, Menu Bar Extra with About and Quit, real UI to screenshot. Watch app embedded in the iOS project (universal purchase); same bundle ID across platforms.

## Art rules (summary; STYLE.md is authoritative)
- Painterly and colourful (high-key, saturated), warm key light, rim light on the fur, amber eyes; slim body, round head, big eyes, both ears upright; slightly stylised, never a cartoon mascot.
- Rooms are 2:1 isometric corner rooms (10 × 10 floor, walls 5 high, a door to the next room), built in code from flat wall/floor tiles projected onto the two walls and floor, multiplied by a painted light map (morning, midday, evening, overcast, night). Furniture sprites come in R and L orientations, all painted in the grey reference room at true 2:1. The cat is a cut-out puppet (painted parts per view) posed and animated in code, handing over to painted resting poses with a cross-fade. The exterior is one painting per home × biome × season, the sky and time-of-day grade drawn in code.
- Never: red bow/collar, white-sclera almond eyes, crescent forehead mark, witch hat, Ghibli greens-and-cream palette. Never name a cat Jiji, Luna, Salem, Binx, Figaro, Felix.

Stored as `assets/world/scenes/<house>_<biome>_<season>.jpg` with `_land.png` (sky mask) and `_glass.png` (window glass); `scenes.json` holds each door point, measured from the door-open edit. Puppet fades render the parts flat (`SKEffectNode`) so joints never show through, and a view change or hand-over fades the incoming picture in before the outgoing one fades out.

- HouseCat is a landscape game on iPhone (Josh, 17 Oct: too much of a portrait screen went unused); the iPad keeps every orientation. Exterior paintings are 16:9 (the square set was replaced) and frame by covering the view without cropping the house's band.
- The room view is the house view (Josh: show what you can expand to, fill the screen): the storey you are on stands on a ground tile for the biome that fills the view (a builder game's base on its field); the home's other rooms sit around the room you are in on the same grid (`assets/rooms/houses.json`, one plan per home), partitions cut down to stubs, closed rooms veiled with their level and price, a staircase in the room the plan says. Upper storeys are shown one at a time with the Upstairs button. Drag pans; the view follows the cat otherwise.
- Decorate (Josh: like moving buildings in a base builder): Decorate shows the grid; press a piece to pick it up, drag and it snaps cell by cell with its footprint green where it fits and red where not, let go to put it down (or it springs back); Turn and Store act on the piece picked up. `PlacementRules` in HouseCatCore decides what fits; each change appends `itemMoved` or `itemStored`. The room's furniture now comes from the save (`placed`), so positions are whole cells. While decorating, a tray lists what is owned but not placed (`HouseholdState.unplaced`); tap a piece and it lands where it first fits near the middle (`PlacementRules.firstFit`), picked up, as an `itemPlaced` event. Paid pieces join the tray when entitlements are wired to the save (`RulesContext.paidItems` is every paid piece, not the player's). The tray's Walls and Floors modes list the room's free and owned surfaces (`availableSurfaces`); a choice is a `surfaceChanged` event, kept per room and kind (`wallpapers`, `floors`; surface ids name their kind).
- Until onboarding exists the home is the London terrace in the city, London time, seasons on Auto (`GameWorld.DemoHome`), and a fresh save seeds the demo layout's furniture granted and placed; the cat comes from the builder (step 2).
- The save sits in the App Group only when the app is entitled to it (no team yet), otherwise in the app's own container; SwiftData traps rather than throws on a missing group. (Since step 5 the app is entitled to it in the simulator.)

- Closed rooms preview what they become: each room type's window and a few of its own catalogue pieces under the veil (`roomStyles` in `assets/rooms/houses.json`). Tap a closed room to see what opening it takes, and open it with Beans when the level is reached (`RoomRules.availability`, `roomOpened`).
- Care is in: Feed (top bar) sends the cat to its bowl with the `feed.*` line; a tap on the cat is a pet (a heart and a purr; six in a row and every cat but Velcro walks off, `pet.*` lines from `CareLines`). Settled for 25 s, the cat picks its next activity from the behaviour table and goes (activities are not saved: they are not game state).

## Decisions during step 2, cat builder and care (18 Oct 2026)
- The coat recipe runs on the CPU with Accelerate (`HouseCatGame` `CoatRecipe`, `CatArt`), not in an SKShader: it is the tint.py maths, checked pixel for pixel on fixture crops and preset by preset against presetsheet.py (`CoatRecipeTests`), and a whole cat (27 parts, 8 poses) renders in under 0.1 s. Tinted sprites are cached as premultiplied textures per look; the same images feed the builder's thumbnails, the profile and (later) the widget poses.
- The cat builder's sprites are bundled by `tools/app-assets/build_cat_art.py` (run after `build_app_art.py`, which now leaves `App/Art/cat/custom` alone; needs Pillow, numpy and scipy): the white painting as JPEG colour plus PNG alpha, the fur mask worked out at master size, pattern and white masks at half size in 16 levels (bled past the outline so the cat's edge never darkens), features and whiskers at full size; about 27 MB. `tools/app-assets/coat_fixtures.py` rebuilds the test fixtures after any art change.
- The puppet is rigged for all eight bodies (`tools/asset-pipeline/rigfit.py`): the short_slim fractions carry over; the breed heads are painted with their ears on, so their separate ear parts are left out; the painted cut faces at the tail root are erased on the tail and furred over on the rump in the app art (`caps.py`). Maine Coon is drawn 1.15×, a kitten 0.72× (Tuning), the weight slider widens or narrows the torso within its set.
- A save with no cat opens the builder (the onboarding's cat creation, without its other steps yet): Body, Coat, Markings, Eyes, Age, then Name and personality; "Bring <name> home" adopts (`catAdopted` + `catLookSet`) and plays the first arrival. The first cat keeps the id `midnight`. Every field is edited again from the profile (Edit look, rename, personality), free.
- The look is in the save (`catLookSet`, `CatState.look`); snapshots from before looks decode their cat as Midnight. A new pattern defaults to a palette colour that shows on the coat (darker, or lighter on a dark coat).
- Eye size and whisker length are not in tint.py: the renderer magnifies the painting around each iris and trims the painted whiskers past a reach from the nose (`Tuning.Cat.eyeSizeRange`, `shortestWhiskers`).
- Names go through `Content/names.json`: `words` are caught as whole words (so "Arsenal" and "Cassie" pass), `anywhere` inside a name after spacing and look-alike characters are taken out. Josh to review the list before launch.
- The gift lands at your feet when the cat comes to rub (or where it ends up, if this cat never comes over), glowing; a tap collects it into the book with its Beans and XP, a rarity sound and the `gift.<rarity>` line. A new level and a new trust tier get their fanfare and line.
- The profile shows the cat's portrait, "Together N days" (streak days since adoption; a missed day, a lost streak or pause never touch it), its age, trust and the days to the next tier, XP to the next level, badges at 100 days and each year, and the lifetime stats (`HouseholdState.profile(of:on:)`). The book lists all 60 gifts, found ones in colour with their count.
- Profile, book and shop sit bottom right; the top bar is the room controls and the status. Debug launches: `HOUSECAT_CAT=<preset id>` skips the builder with that look (a velcro cat called Pip), `HOUSECAT_GIFT=1` with it starts the save yesterday with the cat fed, so a gift is waiting.

## Decisions during step 3, rooms and furniture (18 Oct 2026)
- Run in this session (the app would not start a parallel one from a Remote Control message), straight after step 2.
- Every room of the home is built (`RoomScene.rooms`); the storey on screen is put in the world, the others wait off screen, so the cat can be in a room you are not looking at. The active room is the one you are in: tap an open room, use the room arrows (bottom left) or go Upstairs (into that storey's first open room); decorating, walls and floors, the shop and every placement act on it, and each visit to a room records `roomVisited`. The cat has its own room (`catRoom`).
- Rooms side by side on a storey join through a doorway cut halfway along their partition (`HouseModel.links`, `Tuning.House.doorwayAlong`/`doorwayWidth`); the cat walks through it (`CatDirector.Step.enter`) by the shortest way through open rooms. To another storey (or with no open way) it walks to the stairs where they stand, fades out and fades in on the other side.
- Which room the cat goes to is a Core rule (`RoomChoice`): only open rooms; each room type is for something (the bed at night, the bowl in the kitchen, the garden to watch from: `Tuning.CatRooms.preferred`, taken 70% of the time when that room has it), else it often stays where it is, else any open room that has what it wants; "near you" comes to the room you are looking at. The behaviour table now sees what every open room offers.
- The view goes with the cat into another room unless you are decorating or picked a room in the last 30 s (`Tuning.House.roomHoldSeconds`).
- New rooms open empty, as SPEC "Home and furniture" has it: opening one takes you into it with the decorate tray on Shop. The tray's Shop lists the Bean catalogue, the room's own pieces first, each with its price or the level it needs (`BeanShop`); a tap buys it (`itemBought`) straight into the room, picked up. Any piece can go in any room. Every free and Bean piece's art is now bundled (`build_app_art.py` `catalogue_sets`).
- Debug: `HOUSECAT_HOUSE=full` with `HOUSECAT_CAT=<preset>` seeds sixty days of play (level 13), opens every room and furnishes each as its closed silhouette shows; `HOUSECAT_ROOM=<room>` goes there once the room is up and sends the cat to use a piece of its furniture.

## Decisions during step 4, map, seasons and windows (18 Oct 2026)
- The home's place is in the save: `homePlaced(HomePlace)` (name, coordinates, time zone, biome) and `seasonSet(SeasonSetting)`. Saves with no pin are in London (`HomePlace.london`), as the demo home always was.
- `HouseholdState.homeNow(at:weather:)` is the home right now: its biome, the season there (Auto by its hemisphere, or fixed), the sky at its own local time and today's weather. The exterior painting, the ground tile, every window's terrain and sky, and the rooms' light maps all follow it (`HouseBuilder` in HouseCatGame now builds the house from it).

City names are drawn by code as you zoom. No device location is read. It opens after the builder for a new player, and from the map button in the room (bottom right) to move.
- The biome is the painted mask's (`tools/asset-pipeline/biomes.py` regenerates `map_biome.png` from the land mask with rules by latitude and hand-drawn regions; the painted map is untouched) unless the pin is within 18 km of a listed city (35 km for big ones; `assets/world/map/cities.json`, 245 cities, which the map also labels). - The London terrace's exterior is bundled in every biome and season (32 scenes, about 85 MB with JPEG at 85) and every terrain at quarter size for the windows. Other homes fall back to the city scenes until their own are bundled. Debug: `HOUSECAT_TIME=dawn|day|dusk|night` and `HOUSECAT_SEASON` override the clock and season (with `HOUSECAT_BIOME`).

- Houses: night and snow were already in place (the exterior's lit windows and grading, the winter scenes), so step 5's house work is the widget frames.
